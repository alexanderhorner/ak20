Six experiments for the yearbook: rosettes, loops, a scalloped mount, a wave divider, a margin pattern, and an arc-and-dot composition.

Selected for the book

Rosettes hold the year or a profile number. Loops and small corner shapes add lightness between content groups. Broad, flat color fields separate chapters and bring each person’s photos and anecdotes together.

Built-in image generation supplied the visual experiments.
The selected shapes are implemented as vector artwork in the book.

AK20 / COLOR STUDYDESIGN NOTES

Grouping and spacing.

Sectioning
Steckbriefe, Fotos, Unsere Lehrer and Historische Zitate. Their names and colors match in the contents, running headers and bookmarks.
Proximity
Name, nickname and both photographs share one tinted area. Captions sit close to the picture edges. Biographical rows form a separate group; anecdotes follow after a 20 pt gap.
Hierarchy
Serif names lead. Reading text is 11–11.5 pt; labels are 8.2 pt. The original “Typische Eigenschaften:” becomes the anecdote heading, replacing added filler copy.
Whitespace
38 pt outside margins. Photo baselines and caption positions align. The anecdote panel has 16 pt inner padding. Dialogue stays together, with quote columns balanced by their measured heights.
Motifs
The eight-petal rosette carries profile, teacher or year numbers. Small loops and quarter-circle corners share one restrained visual vocabulary. Background fields have one gently curved corner.
Verification
52 A4 pages; all 215 content units and 88 photo placements checked. 1,063 HTML/PDF text-position matches; maximum drift 0.664 pt. Both earlier editions are unchanged.
